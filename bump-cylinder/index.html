Method & reference

Original procedural interpretations of plain weave, twill, herringbone and tartan, plus diamond and flutes. These are geometric relief studies, not textile simulations.

Inspired by the displacement workflow in BumpMesh by CNC Kitchen. The library and geometry here are generated independently.

Uploaded maps are mirrored into seamless tiles. Pattern width snaps to the circumference. Fine patterns may reach the mesh limit; use the reported spacing to judge the result.
